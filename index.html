Privacy Policy

How Clipframe Media handles data when it operates its own automated video-clipping and cross-posting tools on TikTok, YouTube, and Instagram.

Effective September 22, 2026Operator Clipframe Media

1 Overview

Clipframe Media builds and runs software ("the Tools") that automatically edits highlight clips from video we own or are licensed to distribute, then publishes those clips to social accounts that Clipframe Media itself owns and operates on TikTok, YouTube, and Instagram.

This policy describes what data the Tools handle. It is written for API reviewers, platform partners, and anyone who wants to understand the data practices behind our TikTok Developer app and our other connected integrations. The Tools are internal, single-operator automation — they are not a public website or app that end users sign up for, and they do not collect information from the general public.

2 Data we process

The Tools process three kinds of data, all belonging to Clipframe Media's own accounts and content:

  • OAuth credentials — access and refresh tokens issued by TikTok, YouTube (Google), and Instagram (Meta) for the specific accounts Clipframe Media connects to the Tools.
  • Source video — VOD recordings and episode files that Clipframe Media owns or holds distribution rights to.
  • Generated content — the short-form clips, captions, and hashtags the Tools produce from that source video for posting.

The Tools do not include a sign-up flow, a public-facing web app, or any mechanism for third parties to submit personal information. No visitor analytics, advertising identifiers, or device fingerprints are collected.

3 How we use it

Credentials are used exclusively to authenticate the Tools against each platform's API in order to publish Clipframe Media's own generated clips to Clipframe Media's own connected accounts. Nothing collected is used for advertising, profiling, resale, or any purpose unrelated to operating these accounts.

5 Storage & security

OAuth tokens are stored locally on infrastructure that Clipframe Media controls, are not transmitted anywhere other than the issuing platform's own API endpoints, and are not accessible to any outside party.

6 Sharing & sale of data

We do not sell data. Clipframe Media does not sell, rent, or share the credentials or content described above with any third party, and does not use them for cross-context behavioral advertising.

7 Retention & revocation

Tokens are retained only for as long as the corresponding account remains connected to the Tools. Access can be revoked at any time from each platform's own account or developer settings (for example, TikTok's "Manage third-party apps" screen); revocation takes effect immediately and Clipframe Media deletes the corresponding stored token on its next run.

8 Children's privacy

The Tools are internal business automation, not a service directed at children, and do not knowingly collect information from children.

9 Changes to this policy

If Clipframe Media's data practices change materially, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change accordingly.
